# AcuGuide Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

*Last updated: July 23, 2026 · 更新日期：2026 年 7 月 23 日*

## English

**AcuGuide collects no personal data and runs on your device.** The one exception is the optional hands-free voice confirm — see below.

- **No accounts, no analytics, no servers.** The app has no sign-up, embeds no analytics or advertising SDKs, and makes no network requests to any AcuGuide service.
- **Camera.** The camera coach and locators process camera frames **live, on your device**, to detect hand/face/body landmarks and mark acupoint locations. Frames are **never stored and never transmitted**. Camera access is optional — the atlas and Coach AI work without it.
- **On-device AI.** Free-form Coach AI answers are generated by Apple's on-device model (on supported devices). Your questions are not sent to any server by AcuGuide.
- **Voice confirm (optional).** In the guided locate step you can say a short phrase ("this is my spot") to confirm a point hands-free. This uses Apple's Speech framework. When on-device recognition is available for your language, the audio stays on your device; otherwise that short spoken phrase is sent to **Apple's speech service** for transcription (governed by Apple's privacy policy, not AcuGuide's). It is **off until you enable the microphone**, and you can always tap the button instead.
- **Practice history.** Your practice records (point, rounds, duration, optional self-report) are stored **only on your device** and are deleted when you delete the app.
- **Notifications.** The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device.
- **Children.** The app is intended for general adult audiences and provides wellness self-care information only.

AcuGuide is a wellness self-care app. It is **not a medical device and does not provide medical advice**. For any medical concern, consult a qualified professional.

Questions: contact the developer through the App Store listing.

## 中文

**AcuGuide 不收集任何个人数据，并在你的设备上运行。** 唯一的例外是可选的免提语音确认 —— 见下文。

- **无账户、无统计分析、无服务器。** 应用无需注册，不包含任何统计或广告 SDK，也不会向任何 AcuGuide 服务发起网络请求。
- **相机。** 相机引导与定位功能在**设备上实时**处理相机画面，用于识别手部／面部／身体关键点并标注穴位位置。画面**不会被保存，也不会被传输**。相机权限是可选的 —— 图谱与 AI 教练无需相机即可使用。
- **设备端 AI。** AI 教练的自由问答由 Apple 的设备端模型生成（仅限支持的设备）。AcuGuide 不会将你的问题发送到任何服务器。
- **语音确认（可选）。** 在引导定位步骤中，你可以说一句短语（「就是这里」）来免提确认穴位。此功能使用 Apple 的语音框架。当你的语言支持设备端识别时，语音留在你的设备上；否则这句短语会发送到 **Apple 的语音服务** 进行转写（受 Apple 隐私政策约束，而非 AcuGuide）。该功能**在你开启麦克风前默认关闭**，你也可以随时改用点按。
- **练习记录。** 练习记录（穴位、轮数、时长、可选的自我反馈）**仅保存在你的设备上**，删除应用即随之删除。
- **通知。** 可选的每日提醒为设备本地通知。
- **未成年人。** 本应用面向一般成年用户，仅提供养生自我保养信息。

AcuGuide 是一款养生自我保养应用，**不是医疗器械，也不提供医疗建议**。如有任何健康问题，请咨询合格的专业人员。

如有疑问，请通过 App Store 页面联系开发者。
